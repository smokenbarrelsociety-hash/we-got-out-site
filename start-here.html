Where are you right now?

This site has stories, a glossary, a blog, and resources, and it's not always obvious where to start. Pick whichever of these is closest to true for you today.

I'm still inside

Start with the glossary if you're trying to put a name to something that's been hard to describe. If things feel urgent right now, the crisis resources are the most important thing on this site, not the stories. You don't need to read anything else first.

If you're on a shared or monitored device, consider whether it's safe to be here at all right now.

I just left, or I'm still sorting through what happened

The stories section is probably the most immediately useful thing here, other people describing the same disorientation, the same guilt, the same relief. The glossary can help put language to specific dynamics. When you're ready, finding a therapist who understands religious trauma specifically is worth prioritizing over general talk therapy.

I left a while ago, and I'm not sure it's still affecting me

It's common for the effects of a high-control religious upbringing to surface years later, often around big life events: having kids, a parent's death, a marriage, a divorce. The blog has pattern-level breakdowns that some people find clarifying even long after leaving, and the stories section may surface things you hadn't connected before.

Someone I love is in this, or just left it

Start with the about page to understand what this site means by "high-control," since it's a pattern, not a specific denomination. The glossary will help you understand language they may use that doesn't translate outside the group. One thing worth knowing going in: pushing someone to see it your way faster than they're ready almost always backfires. Patience does more here than argument.
